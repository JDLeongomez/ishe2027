---
title: The 29th ISHE Congress will take place in Bogotá
date: 2026-10-09
summary: The next congress of the International Society for Human Ethology will be held in Bogotá, Colombia, from 5 to 9 July 2027.
---

The next ISHE congress will take place in Bogotá, Colombia, from Monday 5 to Friday 9 July 2027, hosted by Universidad El Bosque.

It is the first meeting in the society's new format: a day of workshops for students followed by four days of congress sessions.

The [preliminary programme](/programme/) shows how the week is organised, and the [Bogotá](/bogota/) pages will help you start planning your trip. The call for abstracts, registration and the congress hotel will be announced on this site.
