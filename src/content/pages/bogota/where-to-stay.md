## The congress hotel

The sessions will be held in a hotel near Parque de la 93. Its name, and any special rates for participants, will be announced here. If you book something before then, choose a rate with free cancellation.

## Where to look

The simplest rule is to stay within walking distance of **Parque de la 93**. Traffic in Bogotá is slow at rush hour, and a fifteen minute walk is more reliable than a fifteen minute drive.

| Area | From the venue | What it is like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicó and Parque de la 93 | On foot | Leafy and quiet, with restaurants and cafés on every block |
| El Virrey and Zona T (Calle 82 to 88) | 15 to 25 minutes on foot | Shops, bars and many hotels. Lively, and noisy at weekends |
| Calle 100 | About 15 minutes on foot | Business hotels along a main avenue |
| Usaquén | 10 to 20 minutes by car | A former village with a colonial square, close to Universidad El Bosque |
| Chapinero Alto, Quinta Camacho and Zona G | 15 to 30 minutes by car | Good restaurants, lower prices and most of the city's hostels |
| La Candelaria (historic centre) | 40 minutes to over an hour by car | Best for a night before or after the congress |

## How to search

- On a booking site or a map, search for "Parque de la 93, Bogotá" and sort by distance. Anything within 1.5 km (0.9 mi) is an easy walk.
- Addresses tell you where a place is. The venue area is around Calle 93, so an address between Calle 85 and Calle 100, and between Carrera 7 and Carrera 15, is within walking distance.
- There is a wide range: international and local hotels, serviced apartments (*apartahoteles*), hostels with private rooms, and short-term rentals. A comfortable three or four star hotel in the area usually costs between USD 80 and 120 a night.

## Before you book

- **Taxes.** Check whether the price includes VAT (19%). Non-resident foreign visitors have been exempt from VAT on accommodation, but this rule is under review, so ask the hotel what applies to you.
- **Warmth.** Buildings in Bogotá rarely have heating, and nights are cool. Ask for an extra blanket if you need one, and check that budget places have reliable hot water.
- **Noise.** Rooms facing the street in Zona T can be loud from Thursday to Saturday night.
- **Arrival day.** Many participants will arrive on Sunday 4 July or earlier. An extra day also helps with the altitude (see [weather and altitude](/bogota/weather-and-altitude/)).
