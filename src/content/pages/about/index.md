## The society

The [International Society for Human Ethology](https://ishe.org/) (ISHE) promotes ethological perspectives in the scientific study of human behaviour. It brings together researchers from biology, psychology, anthropology and neighbouring fields who study behaviour through observation, experiment and comparison, across cultures and across species.

## A new format

Until 2026, ISHE alternated a congress in even years with a Summer Institute in odd years. From 2027 there is a single annual meeting. The congress now opens with a day of workshops for postgraduate students, and continues with four days of plenary lectures, talks and posters.

## Why Bogotá

Colombia is one of the most biologically and culturally diverse countries on Earth, which makes it a natural setting for a society interested in how behaviour varies and what it shares. Bogotá adds direct flights from three continents, visa-free entry for most participants and a compact, walkable congress area. Read more about [the city](/bogota/) and [the country](/bogota/colombia/).

## The hosts

The congress is hosted by the Human Behaviour and Evolution Lab ([EvoCo](https://jdleongomez.info/en/lab/)), part of the Cognitive and Behavioural Sciences research group ([CODEC](https://grupo-codec.netlify.app/), ranked A1, the top category for research groups in Colombia) at [Universidad El Bosque](https://www.unbosque.edu.co/), a university in the north of Bogotá recognised by the Colombian government as a High Quality institution. EvoCo is one of the few research centres in South America that study human behaviour from an evolutionary perspective, with work ranging from mate choice and the perception of faces and voices to hormones and behaviour. One of the other congress organisers is also part of CODEC, beyond the EvoCo lab itself.
