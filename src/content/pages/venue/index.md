## The area

The sessions will be held in a hotel in Chicó, close to Parque de la 93. It is one of the greenest and most walkable parts of Bogotá, with restaurants, cafés and shops on every block and a wide choice of accommodation within a few minutes on foot.

The name of the hotel will be announced here as soon as the agreement is signed.

## Universidad El Bosque

The poster session on Wednesday afternoon takes place at [Universidad El Bosque](https://www.unbosque.edu.co/), the host university, a few kilometres north of the congress area. Details on transport will be added here.
