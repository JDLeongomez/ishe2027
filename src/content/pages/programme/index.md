## How the week is organised

From 2027, ISHE holds a single annual meeting. The congress opens with a day of workshops for students, the format that used to belong to the ISHE Summer Institute, and continues with four days of sessions.

- **Monday 5 July.** Workshops for students during the day. Later that day, the opening lecture and the inaugural cocktail welcome all participants.
- **Tuesday 6 to Thursday 8 July.** Congress sessions: plenary lectures, talks and posters.
- **Wednesday afternoon.** Poster session at Universidad El Bosque, the host university.
- **Thursday afternoon.** A guided tour of Bogotá.
- **Friday 9 July.** Final sessions, and the closing dinner in the evening.

## What is still open

Session times, the list of workshops, the plenary speakers and the detailed timetable will be published here as they are confirmed. See [plenary speakers](/programme/plenary-speakers/), [workshops](/programme/workshops/) and the [social programme](/programme/social-programme/).

Monday 5 July 2027 is a public holiday in Colombia. The congress runs as planned, and the city is quieter than usual that day (see [practical information](/bogota/practical-information/)).
